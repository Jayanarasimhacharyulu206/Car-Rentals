Our Story

Named after a single painted line.

On the coachbuilt cars of the last century, the coachline was the thin stripe hand-painted along the body by a single craftsman — a mark of a car finished by hand, not by line.

The Standard

One inspector. Every car. Every time.

We kept the name because it describes how we work: one point of accountability, drawn straight through the entire reservation — from the first enquiry to the moment the car is returned.

Coachline started in 2018 with three cars and a single promise: the vehicle you're shown is the vehicle that arrives. No substitutions, no fine print about "or similar."

Eight years on, the fleet has grown to twenty-four cars across three cities, but the promise hasn't changed. Every reservation is still handled by the same small concierge team, and every car still leaves our garage with a spec sheet signed by the technician who inspected it.

We are not the largest rental service in the country, and we don't intend to be. We would rather be the one you trust with a car you actually care about.
